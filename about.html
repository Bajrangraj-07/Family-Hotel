About Us

Jai Maa Family Hotel is a family-run dining establishment located in Ranchi, Jharkhand. We serve fresh, home-style meals to our guests across our 8-table dining hall, and pride ourselves on quick, friendly service and consistently good food.

About This Tool

This website is a Table Order Manager — an internal tool built for our restaurant's own staff to take, track, and manage seat-wise orders across our dining hall in real time. It is not a public ordering or booking platform; it is used by our own team to run day-to-day service more smoothly.

It was built to replace paper order slips with a simple digital system — letting staff record what each seat has ordered, see running totals per table, and keep a same-day history of completed orders.
