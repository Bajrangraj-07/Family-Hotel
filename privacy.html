Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jai Maa Family Hotel ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information through this website (the "Table Order Manager").

Information We Collect

This tool is used internally by our own staff to record table and seat orders (item names, quantities, and prices). This order data does not identify any customer personally. If a staff login feature is enabled, we may also store a staff member's email address for account access.

How We Use Information

Order data is used solely to help our staff manage service in the dining hall. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Order data is stored using Google Firebase, a third-party cloud service. Firebase's own privacy practices apply to data stored on their infrastructure. Data is retained only as needed for daily operations and is periodically cleared.

Cookies and Advertising

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. Visitors can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (data storage) and Google AdSense (advertising). These third parties may collect information as governed by their own privacy policies.
